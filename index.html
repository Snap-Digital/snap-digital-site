How we work

Four stages, fixed scope, weekly demos.

STAGE 01

Scope

A working session on users, must-haves and the honest cut list. You leave with a written scope and a fixed quote.

STAGE 02

Design

Flows, then screens, then a prototype on a real device. Nothing goes into build until it survives someone's thumb.

STAGE 03

Build

Two-week cycles, a TestFlight or Play build at the end of each, and a demo call you're welcome to bring anyone to.

STAGE 04

Launch & iterate

Store submission, monitoring, and a first post-launch release planned before anyone opens the champagne.
